Hi, my name is
Artemis

I'm an experimental immunologist and molecular biologist with hands-on experience in RNA molecular workflows, Next Generation Sequencing assay development, and translationally-oriented immunology, plus strong bioinformatics fluency.

  • Learn More

Scientific interests

Systems Immunology

Stucture and dynamics of adaptive immune receptor repertoires, with a focus on T cell receptors, in response to environmental conditions such as infection and aging.

Infection Biology

Immune responses to pathogenic challenges, and elucidation of mechanisms behind the phenomenon of heterologous (i.e. cross-protective) immunity.

Host-pathogen interactions

Physiological and molecular mechanisms driving a host's adaptation to a corresponding pathogen and vice versa, with a focus on tolerance vs resistance strategies.

Adaptive immune system evolution

Emergence, evolutionary trajectory, and role of innate-like T cells along the vertebrate lineage.

Research Projects

Single-cell transcriptomics during chronic parasitic infection

How do T cells respond when we get invaded by a multicellular pathogen? Single Cell RNA sequencing technologies give us a valuable glimpse into the qualitative and quantitative changes that T cells undergo during infection.

  • More

Dynamics of the T cell receptor repertoire during parasitic infection(s)

Use of the three-spined stickleback- Schistocephalus solidus model system to stydy of the response of T cells to infection, as well as the effects of genetic background and sex on the repertoire.

  • More

Effect of aging on the size and structure of the T cell receptor repertoire

Immunosenescence is the progressive decline in effectiveness of our immune system as we age. Experimental animal studies are a valuable tool for us to better understand the nature of this decline as it pertains to T cells.

  • More

Optimization of Next Generation Sequencing bioinformatic pipelines for reliable MHC genotyping

Complex multigene families, like the MHC, pose a challenge to reliably genotype using NGS technologies. Here, we propose a reliable sequencing methodology and propose pipeline parameter adjustments for their accurate genotyping.

  • More

Babesia microti confers macrophage-based cross-protective immunity against murine malaria

The study of the heterologous immunity, based on innate immune mechanisms involving macrophages, conferred by a protozoan pathogen against a lethal Plasmodium species in mice.

  • More

Epidemiology of vectorborne transmission of human pathogens

Reviews and meta-analyses of the incidence of waterborne and tickborne outbreaks of pathogens significant for human health, on a national and international scale.
